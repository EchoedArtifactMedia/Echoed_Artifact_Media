About

Echoed Artifact Media is an independent studio focused on creating media and products that prioritize accuracy, context, and clarity. We serve high‑literacy audiences who value depth over performativeness and seek work grounded in evidence rather than trends.

Projects and Products

Our projects blend research, documentation, and narrative craft to produce podcasts, digital media, and other creative works that explore culture, memory, and the ways stories resonate across time. Echoed Artifact Media operates with an emphasis on intentional workflows, ethical sourcing, and long‑term archival integrity.

Featured Project: Unsynchronized

Unsynchronized is a long‑form documentary podcast that re‑examines the breakup of *NSYNC using historical sourcing, narrative reconstruction, and interdisciplinary analysis. Season One is available now, with Season Two in development.
